THE STANDARD TERMS AND CONDITIONS

    • act at all times in the best interest of Health U;
    • refrain from acting, or being seen to act, in conflict with the best interests of Health U;

    • at all times display a thorough and professional manner, upholding and maintaining the reputation, goodwill and customer relationships of Health U; and

    If, at the end of the Probation Period your performance is satisfactory, your employment will be confirmed.

  11. Termination of Employment
    1. You may resign from your employment and terminate this Agreement by giving to Health U 1 month notice in writing.
    2. Similarly, Health U may, at any time, terminate your employment by giving 1 month notice in writing.
    3. In the case of serious misconduct (including but not limited to wilful misconduct; fraud or dishonesty; conviction for an indictable offence, an offence involving dishonesty, an offence involving the management of corporations; being prohibited from managing corporations; use or abuse of alcohol or drugs (including drugs prescribed for you by a medical practitioner) such that, in the opinion of Health U, you are no longer able to discharge your duties in a proper manner; conduct that causes imminent and serious risk to the health or safety of a person or the reputation, viability or profitability of Health U's business; disclosure of confidential information or other serious or continuing breaches of this Agreement), Health U may terminate your employment without notice. In this case, you will receive only salary up to the date of termination and accrued but untaken eligible leave entitlements.
    4. In circumstances where notice of termination is given by either you or Health U, Health U may, in its sole discretion, elect to make a payment in lieu of all or part of the notice period given. If any part of the notice period is to be worked out by you (as may be directed by Health U, in its sole discretion), the payment in lieu will be made in respect of the unworked part of such notice period. Subject to applicable legislation, any payment in lieu of notice which may be made will be calculated on your salary and other entitlements, excluding any Short Term Incentive payments.
    5. You acknowledge that where either you or Health U has given notice of termination to the other, Health U is not required to provide you with any work and may direct you to perform other modified duties or not to attend the office or contact clients or be involved in any aspect of the business of Health U during the notice period, and that you will comply with Health U's directions in this regard. During any such period, you will continue to receive your salary and other entitlements (excluding any Short Term Incentive payments) in accordance with the terms of this letter.

    This provision is subject to the exception that the period of notice of termination on the ground of redundancy (or the period in respect of which payment in lieu of such notice will be made) will be the greater of: the period of notice provided by the Health U redundancy policy; the period of notice provided by the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth); or the period of notice Health U must give to you as set out under "Termination of Employment" above.

    In the event Health U undergoes a restructure, Health U will meet with the new employer to discuss matters relating to your continued employment, including whether the new employer will offer you employment and (if so) on what terms (including whether the new employer will offer you the same terms and conditions of employment). If the new employer does not offer you employment, the terms of this Agreement will apply.

    You will not be entitled to compensation for redundancy or payment for any shortfall in notice if Health U restructures the business (including an internal restructure or an external restructure where all or part of our business is sold, transferred or otherwise disposed of), and you are offered either a position that you accept, or employment in the same or a substantially similar capacity, on conditions of employment which are overall comparable to your current conditions.
