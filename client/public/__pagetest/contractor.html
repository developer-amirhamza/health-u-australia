THE STANDARD TERMS AND CONDITIONS

The following Standard Terms and Conditions apply to this engagement and to any further services provided by you to Health U unless otherwise agreed in writing.

  1. Parties

    This Agreement is between Health U Australia Pty Ltd ABN 89 661 174 821 (Health U) and [Contractor name] (ABN [ABN]) (the Contractor).

  2. Relationship of the Parties

    The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a relationship of employer and employee, principal and agent, partnership or joint venture between the parties.

    The Contractor acknowledges and agrees that:

    • the Contractor is not entitled to annual leave, personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave, public holiday pay, notice of termination, redundancy pay or any other entitlement that applies to employees;
    • the Contractor is responsible for their own income tax, GST and any other statutory obligations arising from the fees paid under this Agreement;
    • the Contractor controls the manner in which the Services are performed, subject to the reasonable requirements of participants, agreed service times and this Agreement;
    • the Contractor may provide services to other parties, provided doing so does not breach this Agreement; and
    • the Contractor must not represent themselves as an employee, officer or agent of Health U, or hold out any authority to bind Health U, without prior written consent.
  3. Term

    This Agreement commences on [Start date] and continues until terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

  4. The Services

    The Contractor must provide the Services described in the details of engagement above, and any other services agreed in writing between the parties from time to time.

    In providing the Services the Contractor must:

    • exercise the degree of skill, care and diligence expected of a suitably qualified and experienced provider of the Services;
    • comply with the NDIS Practice Standards, the NDIS Code of Conduct and all applicable laws;
    • comply with Health U's policies and procedures to the extent they relate to participant safety, quality of service, incident reporting and work health and safety;
    • report any incident, allegation, injury or change in a participant's circumstances promptly and in accordance with Health U's incident management procedures; and
    • keep accurate records of the Services provided and make them available to Health U on request.
  5. Fees and Invoicing

    Health U will pay the Contractor the fee set out in the details of engagement above for Services properly performed.

    The Contractor must submit invoices Fortnightly, in arrears. Each invoice must be a valid tax invoice identifying the Contractor's ABN, the dates and hours of Services provided, the participants supported (where applicable) and the amount claimed.

    Payment terms are 14 days from receipt of a valid tax invoice. Health U may withhold payment of any amount that is disputed in good faith, or that is not supported by a valid tax invoice, until the issue is resolved.

    The Contractor has advised that it is not registered for GST and accordingly no GST is payable. The Contractor must notify Health U immediately if it becomes registered for GST.

    If the Contractor does not hold or maintain a valid ABN, Health U may be required to withhold tax from payments at the rate prescribed by law.

  6. Taxation and Superannuation

    The Contractor is responsible for all taxation obligations arising from this Agreement, including income tax, GST and any obligations in respect of the Contractor's own workers.

    The parties acknowledge that superannuation contributions may be payable by Health U where a contract is wholly or principally for the labour of an individual, in accordance with the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 (Cth). Where that applies, Health U will make the required contributions and the fee is taken to be inclusive of them unless otherwise agreed in writing.

  7. Expenses

    Health U will reimburse the Contractor at $1.05 per kilometre for travel undertaken in the course of providing the Services, on production of a valid record of travel. All other costs of providing the Services are borne by the Contractor unless agreed in writing in advance.

  8. Insurances

    The Contractor must obtain and maintain, at its own cost, the insurances set out in the details of engagement above for the duration of this Agreement and for a period of 3 months after it ends.

    The Contractor must provide current certificates of currency on request, and must notify Health U immediately if any policy is cancelled, lapses or is materially altered. Health U may suspend the engagement, without payment, while any required insurance is not in place.

  9. Screening, Checks and Qualifications

    Before commencing and throughout the engagement, the Contractor must hold and maintain:

    • a current NDIS Worker Screening Check clearance;
    • a current Working with Children Check, where the Services involve children;
    • completion of the NDIS Worker Orientation Module;
    • a current first aid and CPR certificate;
    • the right to work in Australia; and
    • any licence, registration or qualification reasonably required for the Services, including a current driver licence where a vehicle is used.

    The Contractor must notify Health U immediately if any of the above expires, is suspended, is refused or is revoked, or if the Contractor is charged with or convicted of any offence that may affect their suitability to provide the Services. Health U may terminate this Agreement immediately in any of those circumstances.

  10. Equipment and Materials

    Except where Health U agrees otherwise in writing, the Contractor must provide its own equipment, tools, vehicle and materials necessary to perform the Services, and is responsible for their maintenance, insurance and safe use.

  11. Subcontracting and Delegation

    The Contractor may subcontract or delegate performance of the Services only with Health U's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld where the proposed person holds all required screening, checks and insurances.

    The Contractor remains responsible for the Services and for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor as if they were the Contractor's own.

  12. Work Health and Safety

    The Contractor must comply with all applicable work health and safety laws, take reasonable care for its own health and safety and that of others, and comply with any reasonable direction of Health U in relation to health and safety at a service location.

    The Contractor must report any hazard, incident or injury to Health U as soon as practicable.

  13. Confidentiality

    The Contractor must keep confidential all information of Health U, its participants and its clients that is not in the public domain, including participant records, care plans, business processes, pricing, financial information and customer lists (Confidential Information).

    The Contractor must not use or disclose Confidential Information except as necessary to provide the Services or as required by law. This obligation continues after this Agreement ends.

  14. Privacy and Participant Information

    The Contractor must handle all personal and health information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and Health U's privacy policy.

    The Contractor must not remove participant records from a service location, store them on personal devices or accounts, or disclose them to any third party, except as authorised by Health U in writing or as required by law. On termination, the Contractor must return or securely destroy all such information.

  15. Intellectual Property

    All intellectual property created by the Contractor in the course of providing the Services vests in Health U on creation. The Contractor assigns to Health U all such rights and, to the extent permitted by law, consents to any act or omission that would otherwise infringe its moral rights.

  16. Indemnity

    The Contractor indemnifies Health U against any loss, damage, claim, cost or liability arising from the Contractor's breach of this Agreement, negligence, wilful misconduct, or failure to hold any required insurance, screening or qualification, except to the extent caused by Health U's own negligence.

  17. Conflict of Interest

    The Contractor must disclose to Health U any actual or potential conflict of interest as soon as it arises, including any private arrangement to provide services directly to a Health U participant.

  18. Termination
    1. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving 1 month notice in writing.
    2. Health U may terminate this Agreement immediately, without notice, if the Contractor breaches a material term of this Agreement, engages in serious misconduct, ceases to hold any required screening, check, licence or insurance, becomes insolvent, or acts in a way that causes serious risk to the health, safety or wellbeing of a participant or to Health U's reputation.
    3. On termination, the Contractor must immediately return all Health U property, records and Confidential Information, and submit a final invoice for Services properly performed up to the date of termination within 14 days.
    4. Termination does not affect any right or obligation that has accrued before termination, or any clause intended to survive it.
  19. Restriction on Activities — Non-solicitation

    For a period of 3 months after this Agreement ends, the Contractor must not, directly or indirectly:

    • solicit or induce (or attempt to) any Health U employee or contractor to end their engagement with Health U; or
    • solicit (or attempt to) away from Health U any participant or client of Health U with whom the Contractor dealt in the course of providing the Services.

    Nothing in this clause prevents a participant from exercising choice and control over their supports where they do so on their own initiative and without solicitation by the Contractor.

    This Agreement may only be varied by agreement in writing signed by both parties.
